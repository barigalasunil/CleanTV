# Made with it

People have built launchers, platform ports and add-ons on top of this project. They live in their
own places and are not reviewed or maintained here: read their own documentation, check which TV
they were built on, and treat them the way you would treat any app you sideload.

## Add yours

Open a pull request that adds a short entry: what it is, the TV or platform it was built and tested
on, and where people can read more. The rules are short:

- It is open source.
- It says which TV or platform it was built and tested on.
- It does not collect data or phone home beyond what its own documentation says.
- It is yours, or you have the author's blessing to list it.

A screenshot in the PR description is appreciated.
