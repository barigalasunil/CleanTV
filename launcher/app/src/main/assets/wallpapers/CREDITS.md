# Wallpaper credits

to share. All ten below are either CC0 (public domain dedication) or a work of the U.S. federal
government (public domain). Each one was cropped to 1920x1080 and re-encoded without metadata.

If you add your own pictures, list them here: the file name, where it came from, and its licence.
